ResQBot Mini · Setup guide

Arduino Uno main board + ESP32-CAM camera and Wi-Fi board + DHT11. Wiring, uploading, first tests and troubleshooting.

1. How the two boards work together

Sensors: MLX90614, DHT11, HC-SR04, PIR, MQ-2, battery→ Arduino Uno: reads sensors, finds survivors, drives motors, servos, buzzer, beacon⇄ serial 115200 baud (D0/D1 ⇄ IO14/IO13)⇄ ESP32-CAM: live video, Wi-Fi hotspot, dashboard website⇄ Phone browser: http://192.168.4.1
  • The Uno sends a full status line (all sensor values, thermal map, alerts) every 0.4 s, and an event line whenever something happens.
  • The ESP32-CAM keeps the latest status, serves the dashboard and the JSON API, and streams video on its own server task, so live video and sensor data run at the same time.
  • Your phone's commands (drive, Auto search, E-STOP, scan, acknowledge, beep) go to the ESP32-CAM, which forwards them to the Uno.

2. Before you start

  • Parts: see ResQBot_Components.pdf (you already have the Uno and the ESP32-CAM).
  • Full circuit: ResQBot_Schematics.pdf (7 sheets). This guide follows it.
  • Tools: soldering iron, multimeter, small screwdriver, jumper wires, a zero PCB for the small parts.
  • Software: Arduino IDE 2.x. Boards Manager → esp32 by Espressif (3.x). Library Manager → DHT sensor library (Adafruit) and Adafruit Unified Sensor. Servo and Wire come with the IDE.

3. Wiring, in this order

Step 1 · Power (do this first, with nothing else connected)

  1. Battery pack → DC jack → 3 A fuse → rocker switch → VBAT.
  2. VBAT → L298N +12V and LM2596 IN+. Battery − → L298N GND and LM2596 IN−.
  3. Switch on and turn the LM2596 screw until OUT+ reads 5.0 V on the multimeter. Switch off.
  4. Solder C1 (470 µF, + to 5 V) and C2 (100 nF) across LM2596 OUT. This is the +5V rail.

4. Upload the code

Arduino Uno: firmware/ResQBot_Uno/ResQBot_Uno.ino

  1. Unplug the wire on D0 (from the ESP32-CAM) and switch the robot off. D0 is shared with USB; uploads fail while the camera is connected.
  2. Board: Arduino Uno. Select the COM port and click Upload. (Checked here: it compiles at 64 % flash, 51 % RAM.)
  3. Open the Serial Monitor at 115200. You should see a line starting S{"mode":"manual"… every 0.4 s. This is exactly what the ESP32-CAM receives.
  4. Close the Serial Monitor and plug the D0 wire back in.

ESP32-CAM: firmware/ResQCam_Gateway/ResQCam_Gateway.ino

  1. Take the ESP32-CAM off the robot and plug it onto the ESP32-CAM-MB USB board.
  2. Board: AI Thinker ESP32-CAM. Upload. If it says "waiting for download", hold the MB board's IO0 button, tap RST, release IO0.
  3. Press RST. The Serial Monitor (115200) shows Wi-Fi "ResQBot-01" -> http://192.168.4.1 (video :81/stream).
  4. Put the ESP32-CAM back on the robot (5V, GND, IO13, IO14 only).
The dashboard is stored inside the ESP32-CAM firmware. If you edit dashboard/index.html, run python tools/embed_dashboard.py and upload the ESP32-CAM again.

5. First power-up test

  1. Switch on. The Uno gives two short beeps, and the servos centre.
  2. On your phone: Wi-Fi → ResQBot-01, password rescue123. Android says "no internet": tap Keep connected.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1. The pill shows LIVE, and the camera panel shows video.
  4. No red "Arduino not responding" banner means the serial link works.
  5. Check each tile: IR ahead changes when you hold your hand in front of the sensor; Air · DHT11 shows temperature and humidity after ~5 s; Obstacle changes with your hand at the front; Motion works after 30 s; Smoke/gas says clear.
  6. Manual mode: hold ▲ briefly, then try ◀ ▶ ▼. Lift your finger: the robot must stop.
  7. Tap Thermal scan: the head sweeps for ~5 s and the radar fills in. Stand in front of it: the warm cells should appear at your direction.
  8. Tap E-STOP, then Manual to continue.

TILT_ANGLESAdjust {70, 95} so row 0 looks at the floor 1 m ahead and row 1 looks level.

7. Troubleshooting

What you seeFix
"Arduino not responding" on the dashboardCheck Uno D1 → R1 → IO13 and IO14 → Uno D0 (TX goes to RX). Check the common GND. Unplug USB from the Uno (the USB chip also drives D0).
Uno upload fails: "not in sync"Unplug the D0 wire and switch the robot off during upload.
Camera shows "offline", but the data worksReseat the camera ribbon. The board needs a stable 5 V: check C6 and use short power wires.
ESP32-CAM restarts when the flash turns onAdd or enlarge C6 (220–470 µF) at its 5V/GND pins.
"DHT11 not responding"DATA on D2; bare sensor needs R8 10 kΩ pull-up. Readings appear after ~5 s.
"Thermal sensor not responding"SDA → A4, SCL → A5 (not swapped). Address must be 0x5A.
A command sometimes needs a second tapThe Uno pauses serial for a few ms while reading the DHT11. Mode changes are repeated automatically; drive commands repeat every 0.2 s.
Robot twitches or resets when motors startMotors and logic share the battery: check the fuse, the star ground, C1 and C4/C5.
Wrong motor directionSwap the two wires of that motor pair on the L298N.
