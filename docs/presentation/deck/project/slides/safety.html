SPEAKER 4 · 13:00–13:30 · Back on slides. Breathe; the demo is done. “A rescue robot must never become a new danger, so safety is built in at three levels. For survivors: it crawls slowly near people, stops thirty-five centimetres away, and even if someone drives it by hand, it refuses to drive into anything closer than ten centimetres. For rescuers: the robot goes in first, and it warns about smoke, gas and fire hot spots before anyone enters. And for the robot itself: the emergency stop you just saw, an automatic stop if the phone loses contact for even a second, and a low-battery stop to protect the battery. And we want to be honest: ResQBot is a scout and a decision aid. It never replaces trained rescuers, and ‘no survivor found’ never clears a building on its own.” [CLICK]
