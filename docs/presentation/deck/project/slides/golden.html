SPEAKER 1 · 0:40–1:30 · Slow down here. Let the number land. “Seventy-two hours. Rescue teams call this the golden window. After a building falls, the chance that a trapped person survives drops with every hour they wait. [Pause.] This is not a far-away problem. In 2001, the Bhuj earthquake in Gujarat killed around twenty thousand people — many of them under collapsed buildings. In 2023, the earthquakes in Türkiye and Syria killed more than fifty thousand, and search teams were still digging weeks later. India faces earthquakes, floods, landslides, cyclones and building collapses almost every year. So the question is simple: how do we find people faster — and without putting even more lives at risk?” [CLICK]
