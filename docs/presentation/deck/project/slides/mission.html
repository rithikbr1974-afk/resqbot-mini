How a mission works

One mission, six steps

01 · Deploy

Join Wi-Fi “ResQBot-01”, open 192.168.4.1 on any phone.

02 · Search

Sweeps 140° for heat, drives on, avoids obstacles.

03 · Detect

A warm patch the size of a person, up to 42 °C.

04 · Confirm

Turns, creeps to 35 cm, checks four clues.

05 · Alert

Beacon, buzzer, and a red alert with live video.

06 · Report

Every event logged, downloadable for the incident record.

ResQBot Mini · 2 · The robot

SPEAKER 2 · 4:20–5:20 · Count the steps on your fingers. “Here’s a real mission in six steps. One, deploy. A rescuer switches it on, connects any phone to the robot’s own Wi-Fi, and opens one web address. No app, no internet. Two, search. The robot stops every few seconds and sweeps its heat sensor across a 140-degree arc, then drives forward, steering around obstacles. Three, detect. It looks for a warm patch that’s the size of a person and no hotter than a human body — about 42 degrees. Four, confirm. It turns toward the heat, creeps up to 35 centimetres, and checks four clues before it believes it’s a person. Five, alert. It parks beside the survivor, flashes its beacon, sounds its buzzer — which also tells the survivor that help is coming — and the rescuer’s phone turns red with the temperature, direction, distance and live video. Six, report. Everything is logged and can be downloaded for the incident record.” [CLICK]
