ResQBot Mini · 2 · The robot

SPEAKER 2 · 5:20–6:20 “Inside, two small computers work as a team. The Arduino Uno is the brain. On the left you see everything it senses: the heat sensor, air temperature and humidity, distance, motion, smoke and gas, and its own battery. On the right, everything it controls: four wheels, the two servo motors that aim the heat sensor, the buzzer and the beacon. The ESP32-CAM is the eyes and the voice. It streams live video, creates its own Wi-Fi hotspot, and serves the control page to the rescuer’s phone — all at the same time. The two talk over a single serial cable: sensor data goes up to the camera board, the rescuer’s commands come down to the Arduino. We even added a checksum to every command, so a garbled signal is ignored instead of making the robot do something wrong. That came from a real bug we found while testing.” [CLICK]
