A palm-sized scout that goes in first

ResQBot Mini · 2 · The robot · about 26 × 15 × 20 cm, 0.9 kg

SPEAKER 2 · 3:30–4:20 · Pick the real robot up and hold it next to the screen for a few seconds, then put it back. “Thank you, [Speaker 1]. This is ResQBot Mini — about the size of a shoebox, under a kilo, built only from parts you can buy in any electronics market. [Point along the picture, front to back.] At the front is its most important sense: an infrared thermometer on two small motors that can turn it left, right, up and down — that’s how it sees body heat. Next to it, a camera that streams live video. An ultrasonic sensor keeps it from crashing. A motion sensor checks whether a person is moving. A gas sensor smells smoke and gas. On top, a red beacon and a buzzer to mark a survivor. And the brain is an Arduino Uno. The idea is simple: instead of a person walking into danger, the robot goes first.” [CLICK]
