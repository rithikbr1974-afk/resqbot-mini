SPEAKER 1 · 1:30–2:40 · Touch each card with a gesture (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right). “Why is searching so slow? Four reasons. One — rescuers risk their own lives. Every time they crawl into unstable rubble, a smoky room or a space full of gas, they could become the next victim. Two — survivors are hidden. They’re in dark voids, covered in dust. Many are injured or unconscious, so they can’t shout back. Our eyes and normal cameras simply cannot see body heat. Three — the tools that CAN see heat — thermal cameras, thermal drones, professional rescue robots — cost tens of thousands to several lakh rupees. The people who arrive first, the local fire station and village volunteers, rarely have them. Four — disasters knock out mobile networks and power. Anything that needs the internet or the cloud stops working exactly when it’s needed.” [CLICK]
