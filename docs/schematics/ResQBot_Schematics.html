ResQBot Mini

Electrical schematics · Rev 2.0

Arduino Uno is the main board: it reads every sensor (MLX90614 thermal, DHT11, HC-SR04, PIR, MQ-2, battery) and drives the motors, servos, buzzer and beacon. ESP32-CAM streams live video, runs the Wi-Fi hotspot and the dashboard, and gets all sensor data from the Uno over a serial link.

Pin numbers and net names match firmware/ResQBot_Uno/config.h and firmware/ResQCam_Gateway.

How to read: blue lines are wires, a dot is a junction, purple flags with the same name are connected, red symbols are power rails, ✕ marks an unused pin.
