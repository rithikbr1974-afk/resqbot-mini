Getting the cost under ₹3,000
  • Buy small parts (resistors, berg strip, switch, LED, buzzer, jumpers, zero PCB) at a local electronics market — usually cheaper than online.
  • Use a black drinking straw or rolled black paper for the IR tube instead of the heat-shrink kit (−₹146).
  • A 2WD chassis instead of 4WD usually saves ₹200–300; a single pan servo (no tilt) saves ₹89.
  • Order everything from one store where you can: one shipping charge instead of several.
Before you order
  • Online stock changes daily. If an item is out of stock, use the alternative link or any listing with the same part name.
  • Main board is the Arduino Uno; the ESP32-CAM is the camera and Wi-Fi board. Wiring: docs/ResQBot_Schematics.pdf.
  • Some ESP32-CAM listings already include the MB programmer board; then skip item 3.
  • Check the chassis listing includes 4 motors and 4 wheels.
  • Li-ion safety: charge only with a 2S (8.4 V) charger, never unattended.
